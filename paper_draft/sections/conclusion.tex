\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We asked whether large language models can verbally address novel 2D
geometries presented as text without an explicit reasoning trace, and
whether repeating the same grid in context substitutes for reasoning.
On \addrtask---a controlled $2{\times}2{\times}5$ benchmark over
\gpt, \haiku, two prompting regimes, and five levels of in-context
repetition---the answer is \emph{partially}. Random-access queries
(\qpoint, \qlookup, \qneighbor) are addressable without reasoning at
$\ge 95\%$ accuracy for both tested models, and a small amount of
in-context repetition raises floor accuracy further. Aggregation
queries (\qcount, \qrowmax) are \emph{not} addressable without
reasoning: \Direct prompting fails 12--68\% of the time depending on
the model and query type, and eight same-grid demonstrations only
partially close the gap. \COT closes it almost entirely from $K{=}0$
onward, and the paired McNemar tests confirm the gap at every cell of
our design.

\para{Key takeaway.}
The ``curse of CoT'' generalisation that direct prompting dominates
on in-context tasks does not extend to verbal addressing of novel
geometries; the curse holds for rule induction (which our \miniarc
control reproduces) and inverts for scan-and-aggregate addressing.
Pooling these two regimes into a single ICL bucket misses the effect.

\para{Future work.}
Several follow-ups extend the design naturally:
(i) scale grids to $10{\times}10$ and $15{\times}15$ to test the
\citet{bai2025matrix} degradation curve under both reasoning regimes;
(ii) sweep the thinking-token budget on a model with explicit
extended thinking (\eg Sonnet 4.5) to see whether the
\qcount/\qrowmax failure decays smoothly with thinking tokens;
(iii) replace \qcount with a bounded-arithmetic variant
(``how many more $X$ than $Y$?'') to distinguish counting failures
from arithmetic failures;
(iv) cross-test with a structured representation (PVD-style vertex
lists or SVG~\citep{wang2025vdlm}) as a third axis to see whether
representation eliminates the \Direct--\COT gap entirely.
